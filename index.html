The problem is not access to a quantum computer. The problem is changing the scientific operating stack when the compute layer changes.

The gap today

Scientific workflows are trapped inside tools

Testing a single molecule for a new cancer drug means stitching together disconnected AI tools, software and compute infrastructure. Scientific discovery has become a software engineering problem.

Custom workflow every time

Each experiment becomes a one-off assembly of tools, scripts and infrastructure.

AI recommends, cannot execute

Assistants suggest methods but do not run, validate or preserve the work.

Hard to reproduce and govern

Results live across the stack instead of above it, so audit and reuse break down.
