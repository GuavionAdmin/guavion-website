The problem

High-value work is still procedural. Teams translate goals into sequences spanning multiple software products, scripts, and approvals.

Fragmented tooling

Workflows live as tacit knowledge. Personnel changes break the process.

Slow translation of intent

Manual steps cause delays and silent errors.

No standardization

No unified library of approved workflows—only ad hoc scripts.

Weak auditability

Parameter choices are not recorded; teams can't reproduce outcomes.

Reporting as a tax

Collecting outputs, comparisons, and reports is repeated from scratch.

The Guavion platform

Five core components that replace the brittle glue layer.

Shared workbench

Projects, artifacts, collaboration, and visual interaction in a cloud-backed workspace.

AI agent

Plans, validates, previews cost, executes via audited tools, and explains tradeoffs.

Templates & guardrails

Validated defaults, safety checks, cost ceilings, approval steps, and versioning.

Orchestration

Local, HPC, cloud, and enterprise connectors with step-level observability.

Provenance & outputs

Every run linked end-to-end. Search, compare, and auto-generate reports.
